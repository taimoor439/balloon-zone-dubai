🎈 How Long Do Helium Balloons Last? Care Tips for Dubai Weather

Latex vs foil, heat, sunlight and air-conditioning — what really decides how long your balloons float, and the simple care steps that add hours.

Latex helium balloons typically float 8–12 hours; foil balloons can last 3–5 days or longer. Keep them indoors, away from direct sun and hot cars, and avoid over-handling. If a balloon arrives damaged, our delivery guarantee covers a replacement or refund.
