Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about delivery, custom balloons, and event styling in Dubai.

Do you offer customised balloons in Dubai?

Yes — choose helium, foil, number and letter balloons, personalised with names or special messages, for birthdays, weddings, anniversaries, baby showers, proposals and corporate events.

How long do helium balloons last?

Latex helium balloons typically last 8–12 hours, while foil helium balloons can float for 3–5 days or longer, depending on size and conditions.
